# What's new in Munch Manor

One entry per day, newest first. Only things a player would notice, a handful of bullets at most, written for players and not for us.

## October 29, 2026: How tough are you?

- 💀 Pick Easy, Normal, or Hard, and beat Hard to unlock Nightmare. Waves are tighter, planks cost more, and nothing climbs through a boarded door.
- 💣 Meet the Kaboomer, who brought a bomb to share, and the Big Leaguer, who pitches zombies. Big Lugs wear junk armor now.
- 📖 A brand new menu with the Munchopedia: meet every zombie you've splatted. Your kills and bests stick around after updates, and SETTINGS can link your phone and computer to play from one save.
- 📱 Play holding your phone upright: the manor zooms in up close, and your guns sit on the wall right under your thumbs. Turn it sideways any time to see the whole room.
- 🔥 The parlour got cozy: a crackling fire, a comfy sofa, a big rug (something bit it), and smashed-in doors zombies now shuffle through. Rattles' shop got big pictures, chunky price tags, and a skeleton who cheers when you buy and sulks when you can't.
- 🎯 Aim your way: the cursor is big and yellow now (SETTINGS switches it back), the arrows aim and Space blasts if you have no mouse, and Rattles sells a pricey Laser Sight that shows every shot before you take it.

## October 28, 2026: Opening night

The doors are open. Bring snacks. Do not become the snack.

- 🧟 Eight waves of zombies, a shop run by a skeleton, and Uncle Chunk waiting at the end.
- 🎨 Pick your look: body, hair, skin, and shirt.
- 📱 Plays on your phone too. Add it to your Home Screen for full screen.
- 🔁 New versions show up on the menu, so you never lose a run to an update.
- 💨 Smoother frames when a crowd of Gasbags goes pop, and crisper zombies mid-wobble.
- 💾 The game remembers you now. No more tutorial every time.
