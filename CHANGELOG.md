# What's new in Munch Manor

One entry per day, newest first. Only things a player would notice, a handful of bullets at most, written for players and not for us.

## October 30, 2026: Bring your friends

- 🧟‍♂️🧟‍♀️ CO-OP is here: up to 4 of you in one manor. Share a room code, or hit QUICK PLAY and we'll find you a crew. Revive your buddies before they bleed out!
- ▶️ PLAY is one big arrow now, every menu's close X slimmed down (still easy to tap), your Profile fits small phones held upright, and a CODE card in the MORE hand shows off the game's source.
- 🔫 Upgraded guns keep their names now: Beefy, then DELUXE, then 3000. The SMG is the Chatterbox.
- 🧹 Playtest fixes: Rattles looks like himself in his shop, key hints read the right way round, sprites stay crisp, color pickers line up neatly, we tell you when a browser extension eats your keys, and big hordes run smoother on slower phones.
- 🏳️‍🌈 Be whoever you want: four body shapes, mohawks, buns, beards, makeup, shades, kilts, dresses, rainbow hair, more skin tones, and pride flag shirts for everyone.

## October 29, 2026: How tough are you?

- 💀 Pick how many skulls right by PLAY, and beat Hard to unlock Nightmare. Waves are tighter, planks cost more, and nothing climbs through a boarded door.
- 💣 Meet the Kaboomer, who brought a bomb to share, and the Big Leaguer, who pitches zombies. Big Lugs wear junk armor now, and point-blank shots always land.
- 🃏 A brand new menu: tap MORE to deal your cards, from the Munchopedia to your own Profile, where you strut in a spotlight, carve your name on a tombstone (Snackula, anyone?), and summon your other devices through a portal. It even plays on a plane.
- 📱 Play holding your phone upright: the manor zooms in up close, and your guns sit on the wall right under your thumbs. Turn it sideways any time to see the whole room.
- 🍲 Smash open the kitchen behind the parlour: shoot the ovens to toast zombies, watch them slip in spilled soup, and last ten waves. The parlour got cozy, Rattles' shop got big pictures and a pricey Laser Sight, and your cursor is big and yellow so the dark floor never swallows it.

## October 28, 2026: Opening night

The doors are open. Bring snacks. Do not become the snack.

- 🧟 Eight waves of zombies, a shop run by a skeleton, and Uncle Chunk waiting at the end.
- 🎨 Pick your look: body, hair, skin, and shirt.
- 📱 Plays on your phone too. Add it to your Home Screen for full screen.
- 🔁 New versions show up on the menu, so you never lose a run to an update.
- 💨 Smoother frames when a crowd of Gasbags goes pop, and crisper zombies mid-wobble.
- 💾 The game remembers you now. No more tutorial every time.
